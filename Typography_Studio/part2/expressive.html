Expressive Typography

Exploring emotion and meaning through typographic composition

About This Work

Concept

This poster represents anxiety through repeated phrases arranged in a tightening composition, symbolizing intrusive thoughts and mental pressure.

Visual Strategy

The dense typography contrasts with surrounding negative space to evoke isolation. A clear focal word anchors the composition while spacing and repetition create tension, reflecting the overwhelming nature of anxiety.

Design Principles
Repetition Contrast Hierarchy White Space Emphasis
Emotional Impact

The composition uses typographic density and spatial tension to create a visceral representation of anxious thought patterns, demonstrating how typography can communicate complex emotional states.
